Total assets under management across all 15 funds, tracked monthly since Jan 2023.

Fund Performance

Monthly returns against benchmark, and risk‑adjusted performance across all funds.

New to this? "Alpha" is return earned above the benchmark — positive means the fund manager added value beyond just tracking the market. "Sharpe Ratio" measures return per unit of risk taken (higher = better risk‑adjusted performance). Volatility is how much a fund's returns bounce around.

Cash Flow & Transaction Review

Capital calls vs. distributions by year, and the status of the transaction compliance review.

New to this? A "capital call" is money the fund draws from investors to make an investment. A "distribution" is money paid back out. Comparing the two shows whether the fund is deploying capital faster than it's returning it. The review chart tracks how many transactions were checked or flagged.

About this project

Built the full data model from scratch in Power BI: a 6‑table star schema (fact_performance, fact_transactions, fact_allocations, dim_funds, dim_investors, dim_date) with custom DAX measures for risk‑adjusted performance and compliance flagging, across 4 report pages.
